# 🍬 Swaadika

### Tradition in Every Bite.

Swaadika is a modern digital platform being built for a growing Indian sweets and gifting business.

The vision is to transform a traditional sweet-mart business into a scalable, technology-driven brand with a beautiful customer experience and powerful business management capabilities.

---

## 🌟 Vision

Swaadika aims to bring together traditional Indian sweets, modern gifting, online ordering, and business management into one digital platform.

The platform is being designed with future expansion in mind — from a single store to multiple branches and a larger sweets & gifting brand.
